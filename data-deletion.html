Data Deletion Request

Request permanent deletion of your account and all associated personal data. Quick, free, and irreversible.

Last updated October 21, 2026

Your Right to Delete: You may request deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time, free of charge. We process all requests in accordance with applicable privacy laws including GDPR, CCPA, and Apple App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1(v).

You have the right to request deletion of your SimplTasks account and all associated personal data at any time. Two methods are available — both achieve the same result. There is no cost to delete your account.

Overview

  • In-App Deletion — fastest, takes effect immediately.
  • Email Request — for when you cannot access your account. Processed within 7 days.

Method 1: In-App Account Deletion Recommended

  1. Open SimplTasks — Log in at simpl-tasks.com or your Android/iOS mobile app.
  2. Navigate to Settings — Tap your profile icon and select Account Settings.
  3. Select "Delete Account" — Scroll to the bottom and tap the Delete Account button.
  4. Confirm Deletion — Read the warning, enter your password to verify, and confirm.
  5. Confirmation Received — A confirmation message appears on screen and a confirmation email is sent.

Method 2: Email Request

  1. Compose an Email — Send to: support@thestrategysolution.com
  2. Subject Line — Use exactly: "Data Deletion Request"
  3. Include: Your account email, your full name, and optionally the reason.
  4. Receive Acknowledgment — Within 24 hours.
  5. Verify Identity — Reply to the follow-up verification email.
  6. Deletion Confirmed — Account deleted within 7 days of verification.
Important: We only process requests from the email address associated with the account. We never ask for your password via email.

Data We May Retain

  • Legal Obligations: If required by law, court order, or regulation.

  • Anonymized Data: Aggregated, anonymized data that cannot identify you.
  • Server Logs: Automatically purged after 14 days.
